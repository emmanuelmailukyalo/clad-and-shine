CLAD AND SHINE — PRODUCTION READINESS CHECKLIST

The local cart and ordering flow is a working prototype. Complete these items before accepting real orders.

[ ] Replace sample catalog data
Confirm product names, images, prices, size options, SKUs, stock counts, fit and care notes, and delivery fees.

[ ] Move the database to production hosting
Migrate the local SQLite products, variants, inventory, and order records to a managed database such as Supabase Postgres. Configure access policies, backups, and recovery before launch. Review: https://supabase.com/docs/guides/deployment/going-into-prod

[ ] Deploy the storefront and server
Use your domain, HTTPS, and a hosted server. Keep database and payment secrets on the server, never in browser code.

[ ] Secure admin access
Replace the temporary password with individual staff accounts and proper authentication, permissions, and account recovery.

[ ] Connect payments
Complete merchant setup and sandbox testing for Global Payments and M-Pesa. The current checkout records orders locally but does not take payment. References: https://developer.globalpayments.com/ and https://developer.safaricom.co.ke/apis

[ ] Make order status follow confirmed payment
Handle provider callbacks, duplicate notifications, failed or abandoned payments, cancellations, refunds, and stock release or reservation.

[ ] Set up customer communications and fulfilment
Prepare order confirmations, payment results, delivery updates, returns, and a staff process for picking, packing, and shipping orders.

[ ] Prepare privacy and business policies
Publish delivery, returns, privacy, and terms information. Review Kenya data-protection obligations for customer details and check whether ODPC registration applies to the business. References: https://www.odpc.go.ke/faqs/ and https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2019/24/eng%402019-11-15/source

[ ] Rehearse before launch
Test payment success and failure, out-of-stock races, order cancellation, refunds, admin access, backups, and database restoration.

CURRENT STATUS
The site is a local prototype until hosting, real business data, and payment processing are set up.
